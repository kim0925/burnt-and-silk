Four flavours, one burnt top.

Every cake is a whole 6-inch, from RM80. We confirm the price of your flavour when you order.

    Not burnt by accident. Burnt with purpose.

    A Basque cheesecake is baked hot, so the top caramelises almost black while the inside stays soft. That contrast is the whole idea behind BURNT & SILK.

    We bake in small batches in Klang, and only to order, so your cake is made for the date you pick.

    Pre-order your cake

    1. Choose a flavour and a date at least 3 days away.
    2. Tap send. WhatsApp opens with your order already written out.
    3. We reply to confirm the date, price and payment. Pick up in Klang, or we book a Lalamove for you.

    Lalamove delivery is charged at the actual fee for your distance. No payment is taken on this website.

    Your order is confirmed once we reply.
